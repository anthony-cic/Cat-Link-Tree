Photos of the cat go in this folder.

The site looks for these names by default (change them in CONFIG at the
bottom of index.html if you'd rather use your own):

  portrait.jpg    square, ~600x600      the portrait at the top of the page
  wallpaper.jpg   landscape, ~1920 wide the desktop background ("desktop" theme)
  photo-1.jpg     square-ish            gallery tile 1
  photo-2.jpg                           gallery tile 2
  photo-3.jpg                           gallery tile 3
  photo-4.jpg                           gallery tile 4
  photo-5.jpg                           gallery tile 5
  photo-6.jpg                           gallery tile 6

Nothing breaks if a file is missing - that slot just shows a labelled
placeholder instead. Drop a file in, refresh, and it appears.

Compress before committing: 1200px on the long edge at ~80% JPEG quality
is plenty. Straight-from-the-phone photos are ~4MB each and will make the
page feel slow on mobile.
